Introduction

Hello! I'm Hrishika, a graduate student pursuing an M.S. in Information Science at UNC Chapel Hill, continuing from my B.S. in Information Science and Neuroscience with a minor in Computer Science through the SILS BS-MS Dual Pathway. I study how people find, use, and make sense of information.

My work lives at the intersection of human computer interaction, neural networks, and user-centered experiences. I care about building tools that are accessible, trustworthy, and actually usable. Recent projects include eye tracking engagement in AI assisted academic search, model card evaluation for brain computer interfaces, and a systematic review on how large language models are changing the way we communicate.

Values and Passions

I've always loved science because it answered the questions I asked as a kid, how birds fly, how plants grow, how people get sick, how dreaming in sleep works. That curiosity eventually pulled me toward the systems people use to find, interpret, and share what they know.

I'm deeply invested in STEM education and access. Teaching has shaped how I do every other thing, from research to design. As the oldest sibling in my family, I spent my childhood explaining things at the kitchen table, and I've carried that into tutoring chemistry through SMath Tutors, founding Girls Who Code chapters, and facilitating Python workshops at UNC. I am driven by the conviction that education is a powerful tool for creating positive change.

I ended up in the information science field because I fundamentally believe that--as Dr. Cassidy Sugimoto said at my commencement address--"data and information only matters so far as they help people to live fuller and freer lives." In my Statement of Purpose for grad school, my favorite line I wrote was,

It’s about using technology to make life a little easier, communication a little clearer, and systems a little more human.

Outside STEM I'm passionate about music, theatre, and dance. I've trained in Bharatanatyam for over a decade, competed in Shakespeare and Speech and Debate, and can usually be found writing lyrics or tapping my feet to whatever happens to be playing.

Activities and Accolades

I work on taking taking complex ideas to make them understandable and usable. That thread connects a background in neuroscience and computer science, a graduate degree in information science, and over a decade of explaining ideas at science fairs, in classrooms, and on stages.

I've been doing research for close to a decade, presenting work on biotechnology, nanotechnology, and human computer interaction at ISEF, AJAS, BioGENEius, and the NC International Science Challenge. As co founder of SkyeLabs Innovation Inc. I co-developed EcoGel, a sustainable biopolymer aerogel that won the Pete Conrad Scholar award and earned a NASA iTech Top 10 Finalist spot with the sole Honorable Mention as the youngest team ever recognized.

On campus I serve as a SILS (School of Information and Library Science) Ambassador and Student Advisory Board member, Co President of UNC Zero Degrees, and College Loop President of UNC Girls Who Code. I previously served as Editor in Chief of the Broad Street Scientific, NCSSM's official journal of student STEM research.

Currently: finishing my M.S. in Information Science at UNC while running an eye-tracking study on how students search with AI, co-leading UNC Zero Degrees and serving on the SILS student advisory board, and building out this site and its garden. Lately: re-reading the Stormlight Archive and chipping away at a new song.

Skills

Research and methods: mixed-methods and between-subjects study design, usability testing, think-aloud protocols, survey-instrument design, systematic review (PRISMA, citation chasing, evidence mapping), statistical analysis (Mann-Whitney U, Spearman's rho, mixed-effects regression), Cognitive Load Theory (DCLQ) and the System Usability Scale, and model-card explainability evaluation.

Programming: Python, JavaScript, SQL, Java, C, HTML and CSS, React, Node.js, Three.js, AJAX, and Dart/Flutter.

Machine learning and data: TensorFlow and Keras, MNE (EEG), CNN architectures (EEGNet, DeepConvNet, ShallowConvNet), and data visualization with Leaflet and Highcharts.

Tools and platforms: Git and GitHub, FHIR, Figma, WordPress, Wix, Notion, Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint), Google Suite, Adobe Photoshop and Acrobat, Archive-It, and SEO; familiar with SDLC, Agile and Waterfall, and CI/CD.

Design and fabrication: ChimeraX, Blender, TinkerCAD, Onshape, AutoCAD, and Twine; 3D printing (FDM, PLA and PETG); computational chemistry (Schrodinger Maestro, Gaussian, WebMO, MATLAB).

Focus areas: human-computer interaction, UX research, accessibility, AI and ML evaluation, health informatics, and science communication.

Languages: English and Bengali (fluent), Spanish (proficient), Hindi (novice).
