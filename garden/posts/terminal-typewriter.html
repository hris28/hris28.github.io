Building the whoami terminal on my homepage

20 Nov 2025 · webdev, design, accessibility

This is an example of a hand-built HTML post--the kind of entry where I want full control over layout, images, or embeds. (For quicker notes I just write Markdown; the garden supports both.)

Why a terminal?

I wanted my homepage to say what I do without reading like a résumé bullet list. A little terminal that types out whoami and cat focus.txt felt more like me--a nod to the command line, with a bit of personality.

Keeping it accessible

The catch with typewriter effects is that they can be hostile to screen readers and to people who get motion-sick. So the animated line is hidden from assistive tech, the full list of focus areas lives in a screen-reader-only element, and anyone with reduce motion turned on just sees the complete list with no animation at all. Clicking the terminal also pins the full list open.

The fun part should never cost someone the content.

That's the whole idea behind the garden, really--a place for the cool stuff that still respects whoever shows up to read it.
