A story,
Of star-crossed lovers,
No fantasy,
Only a tragic tale.

Once upon a time,
Isn’t that how they all start.
There was a boy,
With a broken heart.

The Capulet’s and Montague’s,
When near each other a fight brews.

So I call to him and say,
Wherefore art thou Romeo,
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.
Together we’ll run away,
Wherefore art thou Romeo,
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.

He crashed a party,
And found love at first sight.
Got kissed by a beauty,
If only there wasn’t a fight.

A murder brought the hate in him,
Killed his cousin, revenge strikken.

So I call to him and say,
Wherefore art thou Romeo,
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.
Together we’ll run away,
Wherefore art thou Romeo,
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.

They all cry,
Banished,
I would rather die,
He sobbed and said.

He had left,
With no’one else,
Found his love dead,
And killed himself.

So I call to him and say,
Wherefore art thou Romeo,
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.
Together we’ll run away,
Wherefore art thou Romeo.
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.

There was love and there was hate,
Many untimely deaths,
Was it luck or was it fate,
As they took their last breaths.

This is the tale with a tragic end.
The two now rest together.
Many tears have been shed.
The lovers with each other forever.

So I call to him and say,
Wherefore art thou Romeo,
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.
Together we’ll run away,
Wherefore art thou Romeo.
Refuse thy name,
Just don’t leave me alone.
