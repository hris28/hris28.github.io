Grass sways,
Wind blows,
Sun shines overhead.

Children play,
Across meadows,
And trees dance with the wind.

Laughter pervades,
The air flow,
Like a tinkling of a bell.

Another day,
To lie below,
It all.

Below the warmth of sunshine,
And this Beautiful Blanket of Blue.

Try and try as you might,
Still can’t reach that Beautiful Blue.

Below the birds with their wings to fly,
Under this beautiful view.

Below the skies with no clouds in sight,
Making a Beautiful Blanket of Blue.

Sun falls,
Light dims,
Darkness sweeps in.

Sparkling,
Diamonds,
Above the sky they swim.

Connect the dots,
And make a shape of,
Stars.

Another night,
To lie below,
It all.

Below the breeze across my skin,
And this Beautiful blanket of blue.

Below the stars that show time and again,
Shining along with the moon.

Below the lights
Of the fireflies,
Under this beautiful view.

Below the darkness of the night’s,
Beautiful Blanket of Blue.
