AI Scraping & the Future of Digital Preservation Archives

Spring 2026 · INLS 690 (AI Law & the Struggle for Control of Knowledge), Profs. Will Cross & Dave Hansen

Public-good archives like the Internet Archive are caught in a bind: the same open access that makes them valuable also makes them an easy target for large-scale automated and AI scraping, which strains their infrastructure and reopens hard copyright questions. For this project I wrote a tiered access-and-use policy for the Internet Archive, an Open Access tier, a Credentialed Research tier, and a Data Trust tier for commercial model training, grounded in an analysis of fair-use case law (HathiTrust, Google Books, Thomson Reuters v. Ross, Bartz v. Anthropic, Kadrey v. Meta) and a stakeholder scenario analysis. The final annotated policy memo is the main deliverable; the two analyses that fed into it are included below as supporting work.
