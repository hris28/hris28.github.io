Eye Tracking Engagement in AI-Assisted Academic Search

Fall 2025 · INLS 690.267, Prof. Fei Yu · with JaeGoo Ha

This mixed-methods study asks how AI assistance changes the way UNC students actually search for and comprehend scholarly sources. We compared ChatGPT-assisted search against unaided UNC Library Search, pairing Tobii eye-tracking (fixations, saccades, and pupil dilation across labeled interface regions) with think-aloud sessions and post-task instruments adapting Cognitive Load Theory and the System Usability Scale. A needs assessment of twelve student interviews found that 65% struggled with keyword expansion and 38% leaned on ChatGPT without verifying sources; early pilot sessions hinted at a shorter time to first relevant result with AI assistance, though those are preliminary trends only.
